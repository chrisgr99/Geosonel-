# Section 15 — Saving & Score Files

_Status: outline._

How a piece is stored, loaded, and managed across sessions.

## In this section

- **The score bundle** — what a score is made of (the scene data, the script, and any
  image) and how it is held together.
- **Saving** — Save and Save As; what gets written; the dot-prefix unsaved indicator.
- **Opening** — New, Open, and Open Recent; the last score reopening on launch.
- **Sessions** — what persists between launches.
- **Moving scores around** — exporting and importing a score.
- **The JSON tab** — editing the raw scene as a fallback, and the cautions that go with it.
