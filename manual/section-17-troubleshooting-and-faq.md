# Section 17 — Troubleshooting & FAQ

_Status: outline._

Common problems and quick answers. Items get added here as real questions come up.

## In this section

- **No sound** — the engine not loaded (first-gesture / Load Engine), output set to MIDI
  with no instrument, a muted or non-selected object, or a voice that is simply very
  quiet.
- **A script does something unexpected** — reading a value with the wrong name; a callback
  bound to the wrong object; errors surfaced without hiding the objects.
- **Notes too short or too quiet** — duration and velocity driven by a low signal; how to
  widen the range.
- **MIDI not reaching my instrument** — the virtual port, choosing the right output,
  channels.
- **The piece sounds different each run** — something non-deterministic in a script.
- **Performance** — dense scenes, many sources, very high beat counts.
- **FAQ** — short answers to recurring questions.
