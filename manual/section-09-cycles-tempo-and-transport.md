# Section 9 — Cycles, Tempo & Transport

_Status: outline._

How time works in GX2: one master clock, per-object cycles that can run at independent
speeds, and the transport controls that drive playback.

## In this section

- **The master clock** — one BPM for the whole score; editing it live.
- **Per-object cycles** — beats per cycle, and how an object's cycle length sets its own
  tempo against the master clock.
- **Cycle speeds** — the per-cycle speed list: playing a cycle faster or slower,
  reversing it, or halting; how the list wraps.
- **Start and stop cycles** — having an object begin or end after a given number of
  cycles.
- **Mid-play timing changes** — how the piece stays aligned when you change tempo or cycle
  settings while it plays.
- **Transport controls** — play, pause, rewind; what rewind resets; the toggle gestures
  (spacebar, command-period, double-click).
- **Timing accuracy** — the look-ahead scheduling that keeps audio tight (brief, for the
  curious).
