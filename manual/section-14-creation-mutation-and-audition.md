# Section 14 — Creation, Mutation & Audition

_Status: outline._

GX2's workflow for generating and trying out variations of a scene.

## In this section

- **Seed-driven variation** — how a seed produces a deterministic variation of a scene, so
  the same seed always gives the same result.
- **Mutating a scene** — stepping or re-rolling to explore variations; what gets varied.
- **The audition workflow** — the Mutate / Loop controls, the beats field, and a clean
  one-shot stop for trying ideas without committing.
- **Keeping a variation** — capturing one you like.
- **Notes and current limits** — what is and is not yet varied (kept honest against the
  code).
