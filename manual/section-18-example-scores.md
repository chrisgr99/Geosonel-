# Section 18 — Example Scores

_Status: outline._

A small curated set of scores that demonstrate common idioms and double as worked
tutorials. Each should be a real, openable score plus a short walk-through.

## In this section

- **A single cycling curve** — one melodic line; the simplest complete piece.
- **A trigger-collision rhythm** — a moving cursor striking fixed triggers.
- **An image-driven texture** — colour and brightness shaping pitch, loudness, and
  duration.
- **A wandering sprite** — image-steered motion making sound where it goes.
- **A multi-object piece** — several sources at independent cycle speeds over one clock.

For each: what it shows, how it is built, and a couple of things to try changing.
