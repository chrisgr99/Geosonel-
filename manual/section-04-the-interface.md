# Section 4 — The Interface

_Status: outline._

A tour of the window and its parts, so you know where everything is before the
feature sections.

## In this section

- **Window layout** — canvas pane on the left; the tabbed editor on the right; the
  transport bar along the bottom; resizing the panes and popping the canvas out.
- **The canvas** — the live view of the scene; the coordinate grid and origin; zoom
  (View menu, keyboard, and scroll wheel) and the fact that the canvas is always live.
- **The canvas toolbar** — the object-creation tools and how arming/locking a tool works.
- **The Properties (inspector) tab** — the form-based editor for the selected object;
  how bands are laid out and how fields grey out when they do not apply.
- **The Script tab** — where callbacks are written; editor aids (autocomplete, hover
  tooltips, accessibility helpers).
- **The J (JSON) tab** — the raw scene fallback.
- **The transport bar** — rewind, play/pause, elapsed time, BPM, and the message/status
  area.
- **Selection** — clicking, shift-clicking, marquee, and what selection drives.
